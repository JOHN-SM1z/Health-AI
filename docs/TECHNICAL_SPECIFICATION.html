Technical specification · for the CTO

Health AI: AI receptionist, online booking with ID auto-fill, payments and queue

One patient record, one queue and one payment ledger, reached from three places: the clinic desk, the clinic's Telegram bot and Mini App (text or voice), and a phone line answered by an AI agent. This document says what to build, what already exists, what must not be built, and what needs a contract or a legal answer first.

1Summary

Health AI is a clinic operating system for private clinics in Uzbekistan. Walk-in registration with a live queue is the default. Scheduled booking is optional for each clinic. This release adds the following:

  • An AI receptionist on the phone. It answers calls in Uzbek and Russian: address, hours, prices, which doctor works when. It books an appointment through the same booking engine staff use and puts the request on the reception and doctor dashboards. Phone-booked patients get updates by SMS.
  • Voice-first Telegram booking. The patient sends a voice note or types what they need. The bot proposes a doctor and a time for that day, the patient confirms, then pays online and gets a queue number, or chooses to pay at the clinic.
  • "Passport + date of birth, and the details fill in." This works for patients the clinic already knows, and for anyone who confirms through the state's OneID. Section 5 explains why it cannot work for strangers by passport number alone.
  • Online payment through Rahmat. The patient pays and receives a queue number. Paying cash at the kassa remains available.
  • Doctor tools.
    • An e-prescription screen where typing two letters ("an") lists matching medicines by INN and trade name.
    • An AI recorder that turns a consented consultation into a draft note for the doctor to edit and sign.
    • One button to send signed prescriptions, test orders and released results to the patient's Telegram bot.
  • Audit and pilot readiness. This covers the open database findings, release gates, an evidence pack and a staged pilot plan for real clinics.
Six things change the owner's brief
  • Prescription-only medicines go through the national "Elektron retsept" system. Health AI's prescription screen hands them over (copy panel first, integration after the Ministry approves the connection). Its own PDF is not a legal prescription for those medicines.
  • The AI recorder drafts; the doctor signs. Nothing from the recorder enters the medical record until the treating doctor edits and signs it. This replaces the 2026-10-05 decision that excluded AI clinical summaries; the owner approved the change on 14 Nov 2026 (D-10).
  • The AI suggests a direction; it does not diagnose or refer. It maps the patient's own words to one of the clinic's directions (for example therapist, cardiologist, lab) and the patient confirms. Clinical referrals stay with doctors. Urgent wording stops booking and goes to a human.
  • The phone agent says it is an AI assistant. A natural female voice is fine. A cloned real person's voice needs that person's written consent. It must not be used to make callers believe they are talking to a human.
  • Passport + DOB alone is never shown back as personal data. Without proof (the patient's own phone, or OneID), anyone who knows someone's passport number could read their address and phone.
  • MyID is not needed. OneID returns state-verified details without a face check. Phone possession plus the passport checked at the desk covers patients who do not use OneID.

2.3 Open audit findings that block a pilot

From docs/DATABASE_AUDIT_2026-11-14.md (read-only audit of production):

  • Starting a walk-in consultation goes through the future-slot booking engine, so a queued patient can be blocked by working hours or time blocks.
  • Webhook claims have no ownership token, so a slow worker can finish or release another worker's claim.
  • The notification and SMS claim functions accept a NULL limit, which means no limit. Neither recovers jobs left by a crashed worker.
  • Stale data: 9 past "pending" appointments and unpaid payment rows on cancelled or no-show visits.
  • An active clinic with no owner, a doctor role with no doctor record, and stray todos/inbox_entries tables.
  • Leaked-password protection is off.

3Scope, non-goals and rules

In scope

  • Phone AI agent (inbound), call summaries, human transfer
  • Voice and text booking in the Telegram bot and Mini App
  • Identity lookup, proof and auto-fill; OneID
  • Rahmat online payment; pay-at-clinic path
  • SMS for phone-booked and no-Telegram patients
  • Reception and doctor "requests" inbox
  • Field encryption of identity data
  • E-prescription screen, drug catalogue, national e-prescription hand-over
  • AI consultation recorder (draft notes, doctor signs)
  • Delivery of signed documents to the patient's bot
  • Audit fixes, evidence pack, pilot

Out of scope

  • AI diagnosis, triage scoring, AI-suggested medicines or doses, unsigned AI notes in the record
  • Drug interaction checking (until a licensed database is chosen)
  • Controlled-substance prescriptions
  • MyID, face checks, commercial KYC
  • Direct connection to state registries other than OneID
  • Outbound AI sales or marketing calls
  • Family booking (one Telegram account = one patient)
  • Payroll, staff penalties
  • Inventing prices, hours or triage rules the clinic has not set

4Patient journeys

4.1 Walk-in at the desk (the default)

  1. The patient gives passport/ID series and number (for example AB1234567) or JSHSHIR, plus date of birth. The receptionist searches. A known patient's card opens showing name, phone, card number and identity status, never the document values.
  2. New patient: the receptionist registers name, phone and DOB, plus the document typed from the passport in hand. The card is marked "confirmed at desk". If the clinic has OneID, the patient may confirm on their own phone instead.
  3. Receptionist adds services (itemized, priced from the clinic catalog) and the doctor.
  4. Kassa collects cash or terminal payment. The queue number is issued on payment (queue_after_payment per clinic).
  5. The number reaches the patient by Telegram if their card is linked, otherwise by SMS if they agreed at the desk, otherwise on paper.
  6. The doctor calls the patient from their queue, writes their own clinical record, and may order lab tests or refer to a colleague. A referral gives the receiving doctor read access at once, with no acceptance step.
  7. "You are called" goes out by the same channel as the queue number.

4.2 Online booking in the Mini App

  1. Identity first. The first screen asks for passport/ID (or JSHSHIR) and date of birth, with a recommended "Confirm with OneID" button above it once the contract exists.
  2. Proof. The patient shares their Telegram contact. If that phone equals the phone on the card, it counts as proof. If not, a 6-digit SMS code goes to the card's phone. The same neutral answer is returned for "no card", "wrong DOB" and "phone mismatch".
  3. Details appear. After proof, the patient sees their own details (name, DOB, address; document masked as AB*****67) and can correct address or phone. New patients with no card: OneID fills everything; otherwise the patient types name, sex and address, and the card is marked unconfirmed until the desk checks the passport.
  4. Concern. The patient types or records (with consent) what they need: "tizzam og'riyapti", "qon tahlili topshirmoqchiman". The system proposes one of the clinic's directions plus the matching service, and the patient confirms or picks another. Urgent wording stops here with the approved message.
  5. Doctor and time. One day at a time. Today is shown first, with arrows for the next days. Only free slots of doctors who provide the chosen service appear.
  6. Payment choice. "Onlayn to'lash va navbat olish" (pay now, number issued immediately for the slot's day), or "Klinikada to'layman" (slot held; number issued at the kassa).
  7. Confirmation. Only after the backend returns the appointment id (and, if paid, the settled invoice) does the Mini App show the booking and queue number. Telegram notifications follow.

4.3 Voice-first in the bot chat ("simple mode")

  1. The patient opens the bot and sends a voice note, or types. The first voice note triggers a one-tap consent question ("Ovozli xabaringizni matnga aylantirishga rozimisiz?"). Without consent, the bot asks them to type.
  2. Speech-to-text on the allowed host. The transcript enters the same agent orchestrator as the phone line (section 7).
  3. The agent answers questions directly (hours, address with a map link, prices, doctors). For booking it collects direction, preferred day and morning/afternoon, then proposes up to three concrete slots as buttons.
  4. If the Telegram account is not yet linked to a confirmed card, the bot opens the Mini App identity step (4.2, steps 1–3) and then returns.
  5. The agent reads back doctor, day, time and price, and books on the patient's tap. It then shows "Pay online" (Mini App payment page) or "Pay at clinic".

4.4 Phone call to the clinic (AI agent)

  1. The patient dials the clinic's number. The call is routed by the dialed number to that clinic's agent configuration.
  2. Opening line, approved by the clinic: "Assalomu alaykum, [clinic] klinikasi. Men klinikaning sun'iy intellekt yordamchisiman. Suhbat sifat uchun yozib olinishi mumkin. Operator kerak bo'lsa, 'operator' deng." Russian on request or detection.
  3. Questions are answered from clinic data only (7.3). Anything the data does not cover gets "I will pass this to reception" and becomes a callback request.
  4. Booking: direction from the caller's words (confirmed), day, slot. Identity: if the caller ID equals a card's phone, the agent asks only for the date of birth to confirm. Otherwise it asks for document + DOB and sends an SMS code to the card's phone. New caller: name, DOB and concern are taken, and the card is "unconfirmed — check passport at desk".
  5. The agent reads back the booking and books only on a clear "ha"/"да". It then states the result returned by the backend.
  6. SMS confirmation with day, time, doctor and clinic address. If online payment is on, the SMS carries a short payment link on healthai.uz. Paying there issues the queue number by SMS. Otherwise the patient pays at the kassa.
  7. Transfer to a human at any time ("operator"), on urgent wording, after two failed understanding attempts, or when the caller is upset. Outside staffed hours, a callback request is created instead.
  8. After the call: a structured summary appears in the reception inbox, and the booking appears on the doctor's day with the concern labeled "patient's own words".

5Identity and "the details fill in"

5.1 What is technically and legally possible

There is no lawful way for a private clinic to pull a stranger's personal details from the state by passport number and DOB

MyGov, OneID and MyID are not lookup services for private companies. OneID and MyID identify a person who signs in themselves. Direct access to the Personalization Center through the e-government interoperability platform is in practice limited to state bodies and specifically authorized organizations. Even if it were available, a "type any passport + DOB, see the address and phone" form would let anyone harvest other people's data. Passport + DOB is therefore a lookup key, never proof.

7AI agent (phone, bot chat, Mini App)

7.1 Architecture

One conversation brain serves all channels. Channels only convert media. The language model never touches the database. It can only call a fixed set of server tools, and each tool repeats the authorization and clinic scoping a staff route would.

  subgraph Channels
    P[Phone: SIP trunk to PBX] --> VG[Voice gateway: STT, TTS, barge-in]
    T[Telegram text and voice notes] --> TB[Bot webhook]
    M[Mini App] --> MA[Mini App API]
  end
  VG --> O[Agent orchestrator]
  TB --> O
  MA --> O

  O --> LLM[Language model]
  TL --> DB[(Postgres: booking engine, catalog, queue)]

  O --> H[Human handoff: transfer or Telegram admin]
  • AG-01 Orchestrator. A server-side state machine with states greeting → understand → (answer | book | handoff) → confirm → done. The LLM is used to understand the caller and phrase replies. Transitions, required slots and confirmations are enforced in code, not by the prompt.
  • AG-02 Conversation record. Every session is a conversations row (channel phone, telegram or mini_app). Turns go in messages with role patient, ai or admin. Reuse the existing tables and extend the enums.
  • AG-03 Provider-agnostic model. Use the existing OpenAI-compatible client. The model is chosen by an evaluation on Uzbek/Russian clinic dialogues (16.3). Temperature ≤ 0.3. A per-clinic monthly token budget with alerts.

7.3 Grounding: answers come from clinic data

  • AG-04 Facts about price, availability, hours, address and doctors are spoken only from tool output in the same turn. If a tool fails, the agent says it cannot check right now and offers a callback. It never guesses.
  • AG-05 The per-clinic knowledge base holds FAQ entries approved by the clinic: preparation for tests ("och qoringa keling"), documents to bring, payment methods, parking. These are the only free-text facts. Each entry has an owner and an "approved on" date.
  • AG-06 Location answers give the address, a landmark ("mo'ljal") and a map link sent by SMS or Telegram. Directions are never improvised.

7.4 Concern → direction (navigation, not diagnosis)

  • AG-07 Each clinic maintains a routing table: phrases and keywords in uz-Latin, uz-Cyrillic and ru, mapped to a direction and a default service. It is seeded from a template the clinic's medical lead reviews. The existing routeConcern() is the engine.
  • AG-08 The LLM may normalize the patient's words into a clean phrase ("bel og'rig'i" → back pain) and choose among directions the table returned. It may not invent a direction, name a disease, or say why symptoms occur.
  • AG-09 If nothing matches, the agent offers a general consultation with a therapist or GP ("terapevt ko'rigi"). The patient always confirms the direction.
  • AG-10 Wording shown or spoken: "Bu tashxis emas. Sizni to'g'ri mutaxassisga yozish uchun taklif." (This is not a diagnosis.)
  • AG-11 Urgent wording (chest pain, heavy bleeding, loss of consciousness, difficulty breathing, child with high fever and convulsions, as listed in the clinic-approved list) triggers the approved urgent message: call 103 / go to emergency. On the phone, the call is transferred immediately if staff are available. A Telegram admin alert is sent. No booking is offered.

7.5 Conversation rules

  • AG-12 Disclosure: the first sentence says it is an AI assistant. If asked "Are you a human?", the agent answers truthfully.
  • AG-13 Read-back before any write: "Kardiolog Aliyeva Nodira, ertaga, 14-noyabr, soat 10:30, konsultatsiya 150 000 so'm. Tasdiqlaysizmi?" The agent books only on an explicit yes.
  • AG-14 After a write, the agent states the backend result exactly. On error it offers an alternative slot or a human. It never says "booked" when the backend did not confirm it (R-03).
  • AG-15 Languages: Uzbek (Latin speech output; understands Cyrillic text) and Russian. The agent switches when the caller switches and keeps numbers, dates and prices in the caller's language.
  • AG-16 Money and times are spoken in full words ("bir yuz ellik ming so'm", "o'n yarimda") and also sent in text by SMS or Telegram.
  • AG-17 Prompt-injection and abuse: user text is data. The agent ignores instructions to change rules, reveal other patients or alter prices. Calls are rate-limited per caller ID and per clinic. After 2 abusive turns, the agent ends politely or transfers.
  • AG-18 Human mode: when staff take over a Telegram conversation or a transferred call, the agent stays silent until staff release it.

8Voice and telephony

8.1 Speech stack: choose by measurement

Uzbek is the deciding constraint. ElevenLabs lists Uzbek (uz) for its Eleven v3 speech model. Its agents platform uses the languages of the agent's speech model, and new agents default to a newer model whose Uzbek coverage was not confirmed during this review. Aisha (aisha.group) is an Uzbek company offering Uzbek speech-to-text, text-to-speech and a phone agent with SIP support, according to its own materials. Neither claim is verified for clinic-quality calls. Run a bake-off before committing.

OptionHow it fitsRisks

VO-01 Bake-off. Record 100 consented test calls from clinic staff acting as patients (Uzbek, Russian, mixed, noisy, elderly speakers). Measure:

  • word error rate on names, numbers and dates;
  • end-of-speech to start-of-reply latency (target p50 ≤ 1.2 s, p95 ≤ 2 s);
  • barge-in handling;
  • task success (correct booking) ≥ 90% on scripted tasks;
  • cost per resolved call;
  • data location.

The owner picks from the results. The recommended starting point is option C with Aisha components and ElevenLabs as the control. Keep the orchestrator independent so the speech vendor can change.

8.2 The voice

  • VO-02 Use either a stock voice from the provider's library, or a clone of a real person's voice. A clone requires: a written, signed consent and licence from that person (scope, channels, term, payment, right to withdraw); the provider's verified-voice process; and the file stored as confidential. Cloning anyone without consent is prohibited.
  • VO-03 The voice must sound natural and warm, and it is always presented as the clinic's AI assistant (AG-12). The goal is comfort, not hiding that it is a machine. A voiceprint may count as biometric data under the 2026 personal-data rules, which must stay in Uzbekistan. Ask counsel before storing a clone abroad (13.3).

8.3 Telephony

  • VO-04 Each clinic keeps its published number. The clinic's operator provides a SIP trunk to our PBX. 9.3 E-prescription with drug autocomplete

Uzbekistan already has a national e-prescription system

Legally valid prescriptions for prescription-only medicines are generated in the Ministry of Health's "Electronic Healthcare" information system, "Elektron retsept" module. They are signed with the doctor's electronic digital signature (ERI) and written by international nonproprietary name (INN). Pharmacies dispense from that electronic record. The legal basis is Cabinet Resolution No. 570 of 8 September 2025, the Ministry orders on using the modules, and Order No. 3277 on INN prescribing. The Ministry said in May 2026 that the system is expanding, not being withdrawn. The Ministry publishes a form for organizations to apply to connect to the module.

Consequence: a PDF from Health AI is not a valid prescription for prescription-only drugs where the national system applies. Health AI therefore builds the doctor's prescribing screen and patient delivery, and hands prescription-only items to the national module. The first release does this by the doctor's own login; after the connection is approved, by integration.

How the doctor prescribes

  1. The doctor presses "+ Dori qo'shish" and types. After 2 characters (for example "an"), matching medicines appear within 200 ms. Matching covers INN, trade names and common spellings, in Latin and Cyrillic, so "an" also finds "ан". The clinic's formulary and the doctor's recent choices come first.
  2. Each result shows the INN in bold, then trade names, form and strength (for example Amoksitsillin · Amoxil, Flemoxin · kapsula 500 mg), with chips for "Retsept bilan" (prescription-only) and "Nazoratdagi" (controlled). Arrow keys and Enter select.
  3. The structured fields fill: dose and unit, route, frequency as chips ("1×1", "2×1", "3×1", or custom), timing (before/after food), duration in days, and quantity calculated from dose × frequency × days (editable). There is a free-text instruction in Uzbek ("Ovqatdan keyin, ko'p suv bilan").
  4. The doctor can save a set as a template ("Angina — kattalar") and reuse it. Templates are the doctor's own, never generated.
  5. If the patient has a recorded allergy to the INN or its class, a blocking warning appears and the doctor must confirm with a reason. This is a deterministic match against recorded data, not AI.
  6. The doctor presses "Imzolash va yuborish" (sign and send). The prescription becomes a signed clinical_records entry and is delivered to the patient (9.5). Prescription-only items carry status "national e-prescription required" until the national prescription number is recorded.

9.4 AI consultation recorder

Approved by the owner on 14 Nov 2026 (decision D-10)

This replaces the 2026-10-05 product rule (docs/CLINIC_OPERATIONS_PRODUCT.md) that excluded AI clinical summaries, and adds a rule to AGENTS.md. The recorder drafts documentation of what was said. It makes no clinical decisions, and the draft enters the record only when the doctor signs it. On main, AGENTS.md also says AI must never write, read or summarise clinical records. The same approval amends that line when this change lands on main: it gains the exception for the consented recorder draft, scoped to the treating doctor.

Approved wording: "With the patient's consent, AI may transcribe a consultation and draft documentation of what was said. The draft is not part of the record until the treating doctor reviews, edits and signs it. AI must not add diagnoses, medicines, doses, findings or advice that were not stated in the consultation."

How it works for the doctor

  1. The doctor presses "Yozib olish" (record) on the visit. The patient is asked first, and the consent is recorded per visit:
    • the doctor asks, and ticks "Bemor rozi" (patient agreed); or
    • the patient taps consent in the bot before the visit.
    Without consent, the button stays off and the doctor types or dictates as usual. The recording indicator stays visible on screen.
  2. Audio is captured in the browser (laptop or tablet microphone) and streamed to the speech host in Uzbekistan. A live transcript appears with speakers labeled "Shifokor" and "Bemor". Recording can be paused, for example for an examination. It stops automatically after 60 minutes.
  3. When the doctor presses Stop, a draft note appears in the clinic's chosen template. Sections:
    • shikoyatlar (complaints);
    • anamnez (history);
    • ko'rik natijalari (examination findings), only as the doctor dictated them;
    • shifokor xulosasi (assessment), only as the doctor stated it;
    • reja (plan): tests, medicines, follow-up as stated.
    Each sentence links to the transcript lines it came from.
  4. Medicines and tests mentioned in the conversation appear as unconfirmed suggestions in the Retsept and Tahlillar tabs. Each one is matched to the catalogue and must be confirmed field by field by the doctor. Nothing is added automatically.
  5. The doctor edits and signs. Only then is the note saved as a clinical_records entry authored by the doctor, with "drafted with AI recorder" in its metadata. Discarding the draft saves nothing.
  6. The history tab ("Tarix") shows signed notes across visits. The patient's medical card is built from these signed entries, never from unsigned drafts.

13Architecture, hosting and local setup

13.1 Do we need to rewrite the backend?

No. The Next.js server routes are the backend. Postgres functions (the booking engine, queue, ledger) hold the rules that must be atomic. Supabase supplies Postgres, auth, storage and realtime, and runs self-hosted on our own server. What is missing are two long-running services that serverless request handlers are not suited for:

  • Voice gateway: SIP/RTP media, streaming STT/TTS, barge-in. A Node or Go service next to the PBX.
  • Worker: notification and SMS sending, webhook processing, hold expiry, retention deletes, daily reconciliation. Replaces the cron-to-HTTP calls with a process that claims jobs from the database.

13.3 Where the data lives (legal status changed in 2026)

What changed
  • Until March 2026, Article 27-1 of the Personal Data Law required personal data of Uzbek citizens to be processed on servers in Uzbekistan.
  • Amendments that took effect in late March 2026 narrow mandatory in-country storage to biometric data, genetic data and telecom operators' subscriber data. Other data may be stored abroad if the law's criteria are met.
  • Cabinet Resolution No. 415 of 29 July 2026 lists 49 countries with equivalent protection; Japan is reported on it.

Consequences for Health AI. The current production database (Tokyo) may now be permissible for ordinary personal data. However:

  • lab results that include genetic tests;
  • voiceprints or a cloned voice, if they count as biometric;
  • health data, if the rules treat it specially;
  • OneID's own conditions;

may each require Uzbekistan. Recommendation: cut over to the existing Uzbekistan hosting kit before the pilot. That gives clinics and the OneID operator the simplest answer. Obtain a written opinion from Uzbek counsel. This document is not legal advice.

19Added to the brief

Items the brief did not mention that the build needs:

  • AI disclosure and voice consent (AG-12, VO-02). This protects patients' trust and the company from a consent dispute with the voice owner.
  • Call recording consent and retention (VO-07).
  • Clinic-approved urgent list and routing table, signed off by the clinic's medical lead (AG-07, AG-11).
  • Hold expiry for unpaid online bookings (Q-05). Without it, unpaid bookings block slots forever, as the audit found.
  • Fiscal receipts for online payments (PAY-06).
  • Mapping each clinic's phone number to the clinic (VO-05). The multi-clinic platform needs it for tenant isolation.
  • Caller ID can be withheld or spoofed. That is why DOB or an SMS code is still required (ID-05).
  • Spoken passport numbers are error-prone (letter series). Prefer caller ID + DOB; read back the series letter by letter when a document is needed.
  • Elderly and non-smartphone patients: slow speech rate, SMS in Latin, paper ticket fallback, measurement of the SMS-only share.
  • Downtime fallback: paper procedure, and the phone line falls through to reception.
  • Costs: LLM tokens, speech minutes, SMS segments and payment fees per clinic, with budgets and alerts (AG-03, NT-05).
  • Subprocessor list and data processing agreements with each clinic and vendor (16.4).
  • Russian-language support across agent, templates and Mini App.
  • Family booking (a parent booking for a child) is a frequent real request. It is out of scope now; decide when it comes back.
  • The national e-prescription system is the legal channel for prescription-only medicines (RX-05).
  • A trustworthy drug catalogue with a named source and version (RX-02); search alone is not enough.
  • Recording consent per visit, withdrawal, room notice, and fast audio deletion (SC-01, SC-02).
  • A fact check on AI drafts so invented medicines or doses cannot reach the record (SC-03).
  • Allergies on the patient card and a blocking warning when prescribing (DW-03).
  • Diagnosis codes chosen by the doctor (ICD-10), so the history is searchable and reportable (DW-02).
  • No medical content in Telegram message text or SMS; documents open only inside the verified Mini App (9.5).

21Answers to the owner's questions

Can patient data avoid being shared with the government? Are our databases checked?

No system can promise to refuse lawful requests. Authorities can demand data through legal procedures, and the personal-data supervisory body can inspect how an operator handles data. What we control:

  • we collect only what is needed;
  • identity data is encrypted;
  • every access is logged;
  • we connect to no state system except OneID, and OneID only returns the signing-in patient's details to us; we send nothing back.

Counsel should confirm the registration and notification duties.

Do we need MyID?

No. It adds a face check and a per-check fee. OneID gives state-verified details without either. Phone possession plus the passport checked at the desk covers patients without OneID.

How do we connect MyGov or OneID?

Apply as a legal entity to the OneID operator with the system description, callback URL and requested fields. The draft application letter exists in the repository (docs/oneid-ariza.md on main), and the code is ready to switch on.

Can we get an enterprise contract with Eskiz?

Eskiz sells to businesses under contract. Request a corporate agreement in the operating company's name covering volume pricing, sender name, template approval and delivery reports. The terms were not verified for this document.

How do we learn how many patients have no smartphone?

Measure, don't estimate. Kassa totals already show how each queue number reached the patient: Telegram, SMS or neither. Add the phone-channel share and the SMS-only share to the monthly report for each pilot clinic.

Should we write our own backend instead of Supabase?

No. See 13.1. Add the voice gateway and the worker as services, and self-host Supabase if the data must be in Uzbekistan.

How do we run everything locally?

See 13.4.
